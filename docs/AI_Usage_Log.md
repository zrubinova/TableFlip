# TableFlip — AI Usage Log

AI was used throughout different stages of TableFlip's development, mainly to save time, explore unfamiliar technologies, and improve the quality of the final application.

## 1. Technology Selection and Planning

AI helped us compare different technologies and development approaches. We already had a general idea of what we wanted to build and initially considered using Python with Flask, SQLite, and a separate HTML/CSS frontend.

AI helped us evaluate the alternatives based on complexity, suitability for our project, and development time. This contributed to our decision to use Python, Streamlit, and SQLite instead, allowing us to build a simpler application without unnecessary complexity.

## 2. Application Development and Database Integration

We had already worked on parts of the application manually before introducing Codex, with some functionality implemented and working in an earlier version.

Codex was subsequently used to accelerate development of the final implementation, particularly with Streamlit, which was a framework we had not previously used.

AI also assisted with integrating SQLite into the Python application, including reservation storage, database transactions, and preventing conflicting bookings. While we had previous experience with Python and SQL, combining these technologies into a complete working application was relatively new to us.

## 3. Automated Testing and Code Review

AI made a significant contribution to testing and improving the reliability of the application.

Codex generated an extensive set of automated tests, including scenarios involving overlapping reservations, simultaneous booking requests, database failures, cancellation, and no-show handling.

Many of these edge cases went beyond what we would initially have considered testing ourselves.

AI was also used to review the code, identify potential weaknesses, and suggest improvements where necessary.

## 4. Documentation

The project's ideas, requirements, scope, and design decisions were developed and discussed by our team.

AI assisted with elaborating on these ideas, structuring the documents, improving their formatting, and checking that they were clear and consistent.

## Our Involvement and Verification

Our team remained responsible for the project's direction, scope, technology choices, and final decisions. Development also involved manual coding and experimentation before we adopted the AI-assisted implementation.

Throughout development, we reviewed the results, decided which suggestions to follow, and manually tested the application, including reservation creation, table allocation, database persistence, and staff functionality.

We also independently ran the automated test suite, with all 33 tests passing.

Overall, AI helped us save development time and address technical challenges, while we remained responsible for the project's design, verification, and final outcome.
